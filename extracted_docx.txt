SDG vs ESG — what's the difference? SDG (Sustainable Development Goals): 17 global goals set by the UN (e.g., Clean Water, Good Health, Climate Action). You pick ONE goal your project supports, along with a specific target/indicator under it. ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance): A broader framework for how responsibly a project is built and run. You describe your project's contribution under all THREE categories — Environmental, Social, and Governance. In short: SDG = "which global goal does my project support” and ESG = "how sustainably/responsibly is my project designed, built, and governed." A project usually maps to ONE SDG, but should address ALL THREE ESG categories.
