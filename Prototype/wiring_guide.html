🌿 AGRI ROVER PHASE 2 — 100% COMPLETE MASTER WIRING GUIDE

Every single red & black wire for all 4 DC Gear Motors, the 12V Water Pump, ESP32-CAM, L298N, Relay & Buck Converter

🚗 LEFT WHEEL MOTORS (Track A)

OUT 1 Twist Front-Left RED (+) & Rear-Left RED (+) together into OUT1
OUT 2 Twist Front-Left BLACK (-) & Rear-Left BLACK (-) together into OUT2
IN 1 Connect L298N IN1 ──► ESP32 GPIO 12 (Forward PWM)
IN 2 Connect L298N IN2 ──► ESP32 GPIO 13 (Reverse PWM)

🚗 RIGHT WHEEL MOTORS (Track B)

OUT 3 Twist Front-Right RED (+) & Rear-Right RED (+) together into OUT3
OUT 4 Twist Front-Right BLACK (-) & Rear-Right BLACK (-) together into OUT4
IN 3 Connect L298N IN3 ──► ESP32 GPIO 14 (Forward PWM)
IN 4 Connect L298N IN4 ──► ESP32 GPIO 15 (Reverse PWM)

💦 12V SPRAY WATER PUMP & RELAY

RELAY COM Connect Relay COM ──► +12V Battery Positive
RELAY NO Connect Relay NO ──► Pump RED (+) Wire
PUMP (-) Connect Pump BLACK (-) Wire ──► Battery (-) Common GND
1N4007 Solder 1N4007 Diode across pump: Silver ring to RED (+), Black end to BLACK (-)
GPIO 2 Connect Relay IN ──► ESP32 GPIO 2 (200ms Active HIGH)

⚡ POWER & VOLTAGE REGULATION

+12V Battery (+) splits to: L298N 12V, Buck IN+, and Relay COM
GND ★ COMMON STAR GROUND ★: Battery (-), Buck IN-/OUT-, L298N GND, ESP32 GND, Relay GND, and Pump (-)
5.1V Buck OUT+ (5.10V) ──► ESP32 5V Pin and Relay VCC
1000uF Connect 1000uF Capacitor across ESP32 5V and GND (prevents brownout crashes)
