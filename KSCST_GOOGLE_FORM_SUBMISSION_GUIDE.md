### 5. Technical Proposal Narrative Fields

#### Field: Project Abstract / Executive Summary (Copy & Paste)
```
Agri Rover is an autonomous cyber-physical robotic vehicle engineered specifically for localized precision agriculture, automated in-situ soil health diagnostics, and zero-risk digital twin algorithm testing. Contemporary agriculture across Karnataka faces severe headwinds: chronic farm labor shortages, rising agrochemical costs, widespread soil degradation due to over-fertilization, and environmental toxicity caused by conventional blanket tractor sprayers that waste up to 70–80% of applied chemicals.

Agri Rover replaces broad-spectrum broadcast spraying with real-time edge computer vision (OpenCV) running onboard a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B single-board computer. The robot captures 1080p sub-canopy video, transforms frames to HSV color space, isolates foliar threats (caterpillar pests and invasive weeds) via morphological segmentation, and triggers directional 12V solenoid valves for millisecond-calibrated micro-burst chemical delivery, saving up to 80% of active pesticide volume. Simultaneously, a motorized linear actuator deploys electrochemical soil probes (NPK, pH, and capacitive soil moisture) directly into the crop bed while the 4WD skid-steer chassis (dual BTS7960 43A H-bridges) is immobilized by a software safety interlock.

A cornerstone engineering contribution of Agri Rover is its high-fidelity 3D WebGL Digital Twin Simulator built with React and Three.js. The simulator mirrors exact physical kinematics, zero-radius point turns, row lane-lock steering, and telemetry streaming over WebSockets/REST, enabling smallholder farmers and researchers to validate patrol and spraying algorithms in virtual farmland prior to physical hardware deployment.
```

#### Field: Objectives of the Project (Copy & Paste)
```
1. Autonomous 4WD Skid-Steer Chassis Development: Design and fabricate a rugged 4-wheel skid-steer chassis driven by 4 high-torque 12V DC geared motors and dual BTS7960 43A H-bridge drivers capable of navigating narrow crop rows with zero-radius turning capability (R = 0).
2. Edge Computer Vision AI Threat Detection: Implement an onboard real-time computer vision pipeline using Python and OpenCV on a Raspberry Pi 4 to detect foliar weeds and biological pests (caterpillars) in real time at >15 FPS and compute spatial pixel offsets for localized actuation.
3. Precision Chemical Micro-Burst Actuation: Integrate a fluid dispensing subsystem with a 12V diaphragm pump and dual directional 12V solenoid valves capable of calibrated 200ms targeted micro-bursts, achieving up to an 80% reduction in chemical volume.
4. In-Situ Soil Health Probing Array: Develop a motorized linear actuator soil testing probe with electrochemical NPK, pH, and capacitive moisture sensors with automatic drive motor lockout interlocks and georeferenced spatial logging.
5. 3D WebGL Digital Twin Simulation & Telemetry Cockpit: Build an interactive, browser-based 3D WebGL Digital Twin using React and Three.js for real-time telemetry streaming, algorithm validation, and remote teleoperation with hardware safety fail-safes (battery cutoff at V <= 12.0V).
```

#### Field: Methodology / Technical Approach (Copy & Paste)
```
The methodology follows a modular, event-driven cyber-physical architecture spanning four cohesive layers:
1. Perception & Computer Vision Layer: An onboard 1080p camera captures continuous sub-canopy crop video. The Raspberry Pi 4 executes an OpenCV pipeline that converts frames to HSV color space, applies morphological opening/closing filters, and segments targets using color thresholding (Purple mask for caterpillars, Red mask for broadleaf weeds). Bounding box centroids calculate spatial error offsets relative to the rover centerline.
2. Edge Decision & Kinematics Control Layer: Python control daemons process vision coordinates and steer the 4WD skid-steer chassis via dual BTS7960 43A motor drivers. Proportional steering maintains crop-row centering, while differential counter-rotation executes zero-radius point turns at headlands.
3. Precision Chemical & Probe Actuation Layer: Upon threat confirmation, the corresponding directional 12V fast-acting brass solenoid valve (<50ms response) fires a 200ms micro-burst from a 12V diaphragm pump. For soil sampling, drive motors are locked, and a motorized 100mm linear actuator drives electrochemical NPK/pH/moisture sensors into the soil bed, logging georeferenced readings.
4. Digital Twin Simulation & Telemetry Cockpit: A Three.js and WebGL single-page application renders an interactive 3D digital twin of the rover, crop rows, and mist particle cones. Live telemetry streams battery voltage, motor speed, and soil health metrics, featuring an automated emergency halt fail-safe when battery drops below 12.0V.
```
