### 5. Technical Proposal Narrative Fields (NPK Sensor Removed, App & Load Proof Added)

#### Field: Project Abstract / Executive Summary (Copy & Paste)
```
Agri Rover Robo is engineered specifically as a rugged, highly reliable, and affordable smartphone-controlled agricultural robotic vehicle for smallholder farmers. In sharp contrast to heavy industrial autonomous machines ($15,000 to $150,000) that rely on fragile RTK-GPS, LiDAR, and automated path-tracking algorithms that frequently fail in muddy crop rows and dense foliage shadows, Agri Rover Robo completely eliminates autonomous navigation and automated spraying in favor of direct, intuitive human teleoperation via a custom mobile smartphone application.

The system is engineered strictly around three intended core capabilities: (1) Manual smartphone-controlled 4WD rover locomotion with a virtual touch joystick, supporting smooth forward, reverse, differential steering, and zero-radius point turns; (2) Real-time first-person-view (FPV) camera video streaming directly to the operator's smartphone screen with low latency; and (3) Manual phone-triggered directional precision chemical micro-spraying using independent Left, Right, or Dual spray trigger buttons. Powered by a Raspberry Pi 4 embedded controller, dual BTS7960 43A motor drivers, four 12V high-torque DC geared motors, a 12V diaphragm pump, and dual 12V normally closed direct-acting brass solenoid valves, Agri Rover Robo completely removes the operator from the toxic pesticide drift zone, slashes chemical consumption by 70–80%, and delivers a practical field solution at a target cost under Rs. 20,000.
```

#### Field: Objectives of the Project (Copy & Paste)
```
1. Rugged 4WD Skid-Steer Chassis Fabrication (10-20 kg Payload Capacity): Construct a high-traction 4-wheel drive agricultural rover frame using four 12V DC high-torque metal geared motors and deep-tread rubber tires, with calculated torque capacity to haul 10 to 20 kg of payload across muddy, uneven soil.
2. High-Current Motor Driver & Powertrain Interfacing: Integrate dual BTS7960 43A H-bridge drivers with the Raspberry Pi 4 controller, providing optically isolated PWM regulation for responsive forward, reverse, differential turning, and zero-radius point turns.
3. Smartphone Control Mobile Application: Develop an intuitive mobile web/native smartphone app featuring a 360° virtual touch joystick, real-time telemetry gauges (battery voltage, motor current, connection health), and dedicated Left, Right, and Dual micro-spray trigger buttons.
4. Real-Time Video Streaming: Mount an optical wide-angle camera on a vibration-damped bracket and stream low-latency (<120ms) live video directly to the smartphone viewport over a dedicated local Wi-Fi Access Point.
5. Precision Chemical Fluidics: Assemble a chemical delivery circuit with a 12V high-pressure diaphragm pump (4.0 L/min, 0.6 MPa), 2.0L chemical fluid tank, dual 12V direct-acting brass solenoid valves (<40ms opening time), and atomizing brass cone-misting nozzles.
6. Fail-Safe Interlocks & Watchdog Protection: Enforce a 500ms heartbeat watchdog (clamping motor PWM to 0% and shutting solenoids on Wi-Fi loss), battery under-voltage spray lockout (V <= 11.1V), reverse-polarity diode protection, and a physical master emergency disconnect kill-switch.
```

#### Field: Methodology / Technical Approach (Copy & Paste)
```
The methodology of Agri Rover Robo is strictly focused on executing human-in-the-loop teleoperation over high-speed local wireless communication:
1. Smartphone Mobile Cockpit Application: The operator connects their smartphone to the rover's high-power local Wi-Fi Access Point (SoftAP SSID: 'AgriRobo_Net', IP: 192.168.4.1), eliminating reliance on cellular network reception in remote agricultural fields. Port 80 serves the responsive HTML5/React touch interface, Port 8080 manages the bi-directional JSON WebSocket control/telemetry pipe at 20 Hz, and Port 8081 delivers live MJPEG video streaming (<120ms latency).
2. Proportional Locomotion Control: The mobile app features a dynamic virtual touch joystick that maps thumb drag coordinates to linear speed and differential steering values. The onboard Python daemon translates these packets into hardware PWM signals fed to dual BTS7960 43A motor drivers, enabling smooth forward motion, tight lane-lock, and zero-radius point turning.
3. Manual Directional Chemical Micro-Spraying: Upon spotting a weed cluster or pest infestation on the live video stream, the operator taps [SPRAY LEFT], [SPRAY RIGHT], or [SPRAY BOTH] on their smartphone screen. The controller fires the corresponding 12V solenoid valve (<40ms opening time) and diaphragm pump for a calibrated 200ms burst, targeting chemicals strictly to infested foliage.
4. Multi-Tier Safety Interlocks: A 500ms heartbeat watchdog halts the vehicle if communication drops. Voltage divider circuits monitor 12V battery levels, locking out spraying if voltage drops below 11.1V to prevent brownout resets.
```
